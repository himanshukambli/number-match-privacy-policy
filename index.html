Privacy Policy

Number Match

Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Number Match ("the App"), developed and published by the app developer, handles information when you use the game.

1. Information Stored by the App

Number Match stores certain game information, such as game progress, scores and preferences, locally on your device using local storage.

This information is used to support gameplay and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer's servers by the local storage functionality.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, advertising interactions and diagnostic information for purposes including ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and advertising personalisation, where permitted.

The information processed may depend on your device settings, consent choices, region and the advertising services used.

Google processes information in accordance with its own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. Analytics

The App does not intentionally use a separate Firebase Analytics integration. Advertising-related measurement and diagnostics may still be handled by Google AdMob or its service providers.

4. Data Sharing

Game progress stored locally is not intentionally shared with the developer through the App's local storage functionality.

Information processed by Google AdMob may be shared with Google and relevant advertising service providers as necessary to provide advertising and related services.

5. Data Security and Retention

Game information stored locally remains on your device. You may be able to remove this information by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not intentionally request personal information directly from users through registration or account creation. Advertising and data processing for children, where applicable, must follow the relevant platform requirements and applicable privacy laws.

Parents or guardians who have questions about privacy may contact us using the email address below.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights or choices regarding the processing of your personal information and personalised advertising.

You can review available privacy and advertising settings on your device and use any consent or privacy options presented within the App.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
